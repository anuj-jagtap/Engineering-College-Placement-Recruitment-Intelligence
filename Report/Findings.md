# Key Findings

## Overall

- Average placement: 63.13%
- Average salary: 5.78 LPA
- Average internship participation: 54.69%
- Average employability indicator: 61.72%

## Branch

CSE records the strongest average placement at 74.36%.

Civil Engineering records the lowest average placement at 49.34%.

The observed branch performance gradient is:

CSE > IT > Emerging Technology Domains > ECE > EEE > Mechanical > Civil

## Tier

Tier 1 institutions demonstrate the strongest overall performance.

Tier 3 and Tier 4 institutions show relatively similar average outcomes.

## Employability

Employability and placement demonstrate a positive visual relationship
in the dashboard analysis.

## Internship

Higher internship participation is generally observed in stronger
performing branches and institutional tiers.

## Skill Gap

The project identifies differences between skills taught by colleges and
skills demanded by recruiters, highlighting opportunities for stronger
industry alignment.

## Recruitment

Recruiter shortlisting can be improved by considering multiple dimensions
rather than using institutional tier or placement percentage alone.
