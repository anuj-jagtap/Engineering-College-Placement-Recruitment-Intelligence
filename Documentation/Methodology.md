# Methodology

## Step 1 — Data Collection

Engineering college information was collected through web scraping from
government official websites.

## Step 2 — Data Cleaning

The collected data was inspected and standardized.

Major cleaning activities included:

- Missing-value checking
- Duplicate detection
- Data-type validation
- State standardization
- Branch standardization
- Category standardization
- Ownership standardization
- Tier classification

## Step 3 — Analytical Dataset

After cleaning and validation, the final analytical dataset contained:

22,386 records × 20 variables.

## Step 4 — Exploratory Data Analysis

Descriptive and comparative analysis was performed across:

- Branch
- Tier
- State
- Ownership
- Category

## Step 5 — Skill Analysis

Descriptive skill-related fields were analyzed to understand:

- Skills taught
- Skills demanded
- Missing skills
- Industry requirements
- Emerging technologies

## Step 6 — Recruiter Readiness

A multidimensional recruiter-readiness framework was developed using:

- Placement
- Employability
- Internship participation

## Step 7 — Business Intelligence

The analytical dataset was imported into Power BI.

DAX measures, interactive filters and visualizations were used to develop
four business-focused dashboards.

## Step 8 — Interpretation

The dashboard results were interpreted from the perspective of:

- Recruiters
- Engineering colleges
- Students
- Academic decision-makers
