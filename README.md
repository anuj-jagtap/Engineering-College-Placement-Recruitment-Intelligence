## Project Overview

Engineering college placement outcomes vary considerably across branches,
institutional tiers, states and institutional characteristics.

This project investigates the factors associated with engineering college
placement performance by combining:

- Placement performance
- Average graduate salary
- Internship participation
- Employability
- Institutional tier
- Engineering branch
- State
- Ownership
- Institution category
- Recruiter demand
- Industry-required skills
- Skills taught by colleges
- Missing skill gaps
- Emerging technology requirements

The objective is to transform raw engineering-college information into
actionable intelligence for:

**Recruiters | Colleges | Students | Academic Decision-Makers**

---

# Business Problem

Engineering colleges may differ significantly in their placement outcomes,
industry exposure and graduate employability.

However, placement percentage alone does not provide enough information
for recruiters to evaluate institutions.

This project therefore addresses the following question:

> **Why do engineering college placement outcomes remain low in certain
> branches and institutions, and how can data help recruiters identify
> stronger and more industry-ready colleges?**

---

# Key Business Questions

### Overall Performance

- What is the overall placement performance of engineering colleges?
- What is the average salary offered to graduates?
- How does internship participation relate to employability?
- Is employability associated with placement performance?

### Branch Analysis

- Which engineering branches have the strongest placement outcomes?
- Which branches have weaker employment outcomes?
- How do salary and internship participation vary by branch?

### Institutional Tier Analysis

- How does placement performance differ across Tier 1–Tier 4 institutions?
- Does institutional tier strongly influence recruiter readiness?
- Are Tier 3 and Tier 4 institutions performing similarly?

### State Analysis

- Which states demonstrate stronger placement outcomes?
- How does employability vary geographically?
- Are there regional differences in recruiter opportunity?

### Skill Gap Analysis

- What skills are demanded by recruiters?
- Which skills are missing from engineering colleges?
- How well do college-taught skills align with industry requirements?
- Which emerging technologies are becoming important?

### Recruiter Shortlisting

- Which colleges demonstrate stronger recruiter readiness?
- Which institutions may require additional screening?
- Can recruiters shortlist institutions using multiple performance indicators?
